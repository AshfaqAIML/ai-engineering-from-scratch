What is Embedding?

A learned mapping from discrete items (words, images, users) to dense vectors in continuous space, where similar items end up close together

What is the common confusion about Embedding?

Similarity depends on the model, training objective, and metric. Distance in one embedding space does not carry over to another.

Why is it called Embedding?

The items are placed, or embedded, in a geometric representation space.
