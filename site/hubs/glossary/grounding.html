What is Grounding?

Connecting a generated answer or action to evidence, state, or observations that the system can identify and check.

Why does Grounding matter?

Grounding gives the system a basis beyond unconstrained generation and makes unsupported claims easier to detect.

Grounding in practice

Retrieve a policy section, require the answer to cite it, and reject claims that the cited passage does not support.

What is the common confusion about Grounding?

Adding documents to a prompt creates an opportunity for grounding. It does not guarantee the model will use them correctly.
