# What is Verification Gate?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/verification-gate
Category: Evaluation & safety

A control point that blocks progress until defined evidence satisfies a correctness or quality criterion.

## Why does Verification Gate matter?

It converts a model's claim of completion into an evidence-backed decision.

## What is the common confusion about Verification Gate?

Verification checks whether evidence meets criteria. Approval grants authority to proceed, even when the evidence is already known.

## Verification Gate in practice

Prevent a coding task from completing until the patch applies, scoped tests pass, forbidden files remain unchanged, and required artifacts exist.
