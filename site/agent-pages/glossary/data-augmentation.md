# What is Data Augmentation?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/data-augmentation
Category: Math & training

Creating modified examples, such as transformed images, perturbed audio, or paraphrased text, to increase training diversity without collecting entirely new source data. It can reduce overfitting when the transformation preserves the task signal.

What people say: "Making more training data."

## What is the common confusion about Data Augmentation?

An augmentation must preserve the target label or behavior you want the model to learn.
