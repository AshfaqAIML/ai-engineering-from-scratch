# What is Cross-Entropy?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/cross-entropy
Category: Math & training

A loss based on the negative log probability assigned to the target outcome. In next-token training, it penalizes the model when it assigns low probability to the observed next token.

What people say: "The classification loss."

## What is the common confusion about Cross-Entropy?

Perplexity is the exponentiated average cross-entropy only when the averaging and logarithm base are defined consistently.
