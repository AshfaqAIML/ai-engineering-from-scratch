# What is AI Risk Assessment?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/ai-risk-assessment
Category: Security & governance

A documented analysis of how an AI system can affect people, organizations, and environments, including context, hazards, likelihood, impact, controls, residual risk, and monitoring responsibilities.

## Why does AI Risk Assessment matter?

Model capability alone does not determine risk. Deployment context, affected groups, human authority, data, and system integrations change both the harms and the controls required.

## What is the common confusion about AI Risk Assessment?

A risk assessment supports a decision under stated assumptions. It is not a one-time safety certificate or proof that every hazard has been found.

## AI Risk Assessment in practice

Define the intended use and affected parties, identify credible failure and misuse scenarios, assign owners to controls, record residual risk, and set review triggers for material changes.
