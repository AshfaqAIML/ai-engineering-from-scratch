# What is Latent Space?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/latent-space
Category: Data & representations

A learned representation space whose coordinates encode factors useful to a model. It may be lower-dimensional than the input, but compression is not required for every latent representation.

What people say: "A model's hidden representation space."

## What is the common confusion about Latent Space?

Nearby points are only meaningfully similar according to what the model and training objective learned.
