# What is Cosine Similarity?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/cosine-similarity
Category: Data & representations

The normalized dot product of two vectors. It compares their direction rather than their magnitude and ranges from -1 to 1 for real-valued vectors.

What people say: "How similar two vectors are."

## What is the common confusion about Cosine Similarity?

High cosine similarity only has meaning relative to the embedding model and the data distribution. It does not prove factual or semantic equivalence.
