# What is Epoch?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/epoch
Category: Math & training

One traversal of the defined training dataset. In distributed or sampled training, the exact implementation of an epoch depends on the data loader and sampling policy.

What people say: "One pass through the training data."

## What is the common confusion about Epoch?

More epochs do not guarantee better generalization; evaluate on held-out data.
