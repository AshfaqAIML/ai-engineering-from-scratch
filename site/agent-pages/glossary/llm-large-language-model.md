# What is LLM (Large Language Model)?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/llm-large-language-model
Category: Models & inference

A language model with enough capacity and broad training to perform many language tasks through prompting or adaptation. Most current LLMs use transformer architectures and sequence-prediction objectives, but size thresholds, data sources, and training recipes vary.

What people say: "The brain of an AI application."

## What is the common confusion about LLM (Large Language Model)?

An LLM is a model component. Tools, retrieval, state, policies, and product logic live in the surrounding system.
