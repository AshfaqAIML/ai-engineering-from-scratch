# What is Transfer Learning?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/transfer-learning
Category: Math & training

Starting from representations or parameters learned on one data distribution or objective and adapting them for another. The transferable components and update strategy depend on architecture and task.

What people say: "Reusing a pretrained model for a new task."

## What is the common confusion about Transfer Learning?

Transfer is not limited to later layers, and successful transfer is not guaranteed when source and target tasks differ sharply.
