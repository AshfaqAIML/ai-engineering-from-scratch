# What is Service Level Objective (SLO)?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/service-level-objective-slo
Category: Reliability & operations

A target range or threshold for a service-level indicator over a stated population and measurement window.

## Why does Service Level Objective (SLO) matter?

It translates an expected user outcome into an operating boundary for monitoring, capacity, release risk, and incident decisions.

## What is the common confusion about Service Level Objective (SLO)?

An SLO is an internal reliability objective. A contractual service-level agreement can include remedies and may use different definitions.

## Service Level Objective (SLO) in practice

Choose an indicator users care about, set the target from product needs rather than current performance, define the window and exclusions, and attach an error-budget policy.
