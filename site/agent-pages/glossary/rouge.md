# What is ROUGE?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/rouge
Category: Evaluation & safety

A family of metrics that compares generated text with reference text using units such as n-gram overlap or longest common subsequence.

What people say: "A reference-overlap metric often used for summaries."

## What is the common confusion about ROUGE?

Surface overlap can miss semantic equivalence and can reward copied wording without proving factual quality.
