# What is Availability?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/availability
Category: Reliability & operations

The proportion of eligible service interactions or time windows in which users can obtain the defined acceptable service under a stated measurement boundary.

## Why does Availability matter?

A service can be running while users still cannot complete useful requests, so availability must be tied to user-visible success rather than process uptime alone.

## What is the common confusion about Availability?

Availability is one reliability outcome. It does not describe latency, correctness, safety, or the experience of every user segment.

## Availability in practice

Define eligible events and acceptable outcomes, exclude only documented cases, calculate the indicator over a fixed window, and investigate both total failures and prolonged partial degradation.
