# What is Loss Function?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/loss-function
Category: Math & training

An objective that maps predictions and targets, sometimes with regularization terms, to a value optimization tries to reduce. The loss determines which errors training directly rewards or penalizes.

What people say: "A number that measures training error."

## What is the common confusion about Loss Function?

A low training loss does not guarantee useful, safe, or generalizable behavior on production tasks.
