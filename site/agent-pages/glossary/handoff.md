# What is Handoff?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/handoff
Category: AI-native development

A structured transfer of a task between people or agents that preserves the objective, current state, evidence, decisions, constraints, and remaining work.

## Why does Handoff matter?

A good handoff prevents the next worker from reconstructing the entire task from a long transcript or repeating completed actions.

## What is the common confusion about Handoff?

A summary says what happened. A handoff also says what state is authoritative and what should happen next.

## Handoff in practice

Pass the accepted plan, changed files, test commands and results, unresolved risks, and exact next action in a compact task packet.
