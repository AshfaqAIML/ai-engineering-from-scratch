# What is Service Level Indicator (SLI)?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/service-level-indicator-sli
Category: Reliability & operations

A quantitative measure of service behavior at a defined user-relevant boundary, such as successful request ratio or latency below a threshold.

## Why does Service Level Indicator (SLI) matter?

Reliability discussions become actionable only when the observed behavior, eligible events, and measurement point are explicit.

## What is the common confusion about Service Level Indicator (SLI)?

An SLI is the measurement. An SLO is the target applied to that measurement over a defined period.

## Service Level Indicator (SLI) in practice

Define numerator, denominator, exclusions, data source, and aggregation window, then validate that the indicator tracks an outcome users actually experience.
