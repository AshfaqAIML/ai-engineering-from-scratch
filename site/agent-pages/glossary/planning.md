# What is Planning?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/planning
Category: Agents & tools

Constructing, selecting, or revising a sequence of actions and dependencies intended to move from the current state to a goal.

## Why does Planning matter?

Explicit plans make assumptions and ordering visible before an agent commits to expensive or irreversible actions.

## What is the common confusion about Planning?

A generated plan is a proposal, not proof that the steps are feasible, sufficient, or safe.

## Planning in practice

Ask for a short dependency-aware plan, validate it against available tools and permissions, then re-plan when observations invalidate an assumption.
