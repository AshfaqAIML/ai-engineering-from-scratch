# What are Precision & Recall?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/precision-recall
Category: Evaluation & safety

Precision asks how many flagged items were correct; recall asks how many relevant items were found. When you change the decision threshold for one fixed scoring model, improving recall often lowers precision and vice versa. A better model can improve both. F1 is their harmonic mean.

What people say: "Two metrics for classification or retrieval quality."

## What is the common confusion about Precision & Recall?

The right threshold and metric depend on the cost of each error and the prevalence of the target class.
