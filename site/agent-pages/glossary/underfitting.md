# What is Underfitting?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/underfitting
Category: Math & training

A model or training setup has insufficient effective capacity, optimization, features, or training signal to capture useful patterns in the training data.

What people say: "The model cannot fit the training task well enough."

## Underfitting in practice

Diagnose data and optimization first, then consider training longer, changing features, reducing excessive regularization, or increasing suitable capacity.
