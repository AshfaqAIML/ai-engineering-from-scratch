# What is Datasheet for Datasets?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/datasheet-for-datasets
Category: Security & governance

Structured documentation of a dataset's motivation, composition, collection process, preprocessing, uses, distribution, maintenance, and known limitations.

## Why does Datasheet for Datasets matter?

A dataset is not safe or suitable merely because it is available. Downstream builders need evidence about how it was created and where its assumptions break.

## What is the common confusion about Datasheet for Datasets?

A datasheet documents evidence and intended use. It is not a license, quality guarantee, or substitute for deployment-specific evaluation.

## Datasheet for Datasets in practice

Publish the datasheet with a versioned dataset, identify who can answer questions, record excluded populations and transformations, and update the document when the dataset changes.
