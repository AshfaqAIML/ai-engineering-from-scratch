# What is SFT (Supervised Fine-Tuning)?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/sft-supervised-fine-tuning
Category: Math & training

Fine-tuning a pretrained model on paired inputs and desired responses so it learns the demonstrated behavior under the training distribution.

What people say: "Training on example inputs and desired outputs."

## What is the common confusion about SFT (Supervised Fine-Tuning)?

SFT can adapt many behaviors beyond chat, and example quality determines what behavior is reinforced.
