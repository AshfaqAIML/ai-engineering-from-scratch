# What is Inference?

Canonical page: https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/glossary/inference
Category: Models & inference

Executing a trained model to produce predictions, scores, embeddings, or generated tokens without performing an ordinary training update to its parameters.

What people say: "Running a trained model."

## What is the common confusion about Inference?

An application can update caches, conversation state, or external memory during inference even though model weights stay unchanged.
