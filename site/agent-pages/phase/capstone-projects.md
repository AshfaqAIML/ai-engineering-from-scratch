A 7B-parameter model carries 56 GB of optimiser state. ZeRO stage 1 shards that across N ranks; each rank owns 1/N of the optimiser. (Capstone, Python, ~90 min)
79. [Pipeline Parallel and Bubble Analysis](https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/lesson?path=phases%2F19-capstone-projects%2F79-pipeline-parallel): Tensor parallelism splits the matrix multiply across ranks. Pipeline parallelism splits the model across ranks, one stage per rank. Microbatches flow through the pipeline. (Capstone, Python, ~90 min)
80. [Sharded Checkpoint and Atomic Resume](https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/lesson?path=phases%2F19-capstone-projects%2F80-checkpoint-sharded-resume): A 70B-parameter training job is paused by a node failure every few hours. The checkpoint format decides whether you lose 30 minutes or 30 hours. (Capstone, Python, ~90 min)
81. [End-to-End Distributed Training](https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/lesson?path=phases%2F19-capstone-projects%2F81-end-to-end-distributed-train): Lessons 76 through 80 each built one piece. This is the assembly: a tiny GPT trained across 4 simulated ranks with DDP for gradient sync, ZeRO-1 for optimiser-state sharding, and a sharded… (Capstone, Python, ~90 min)
82. [Capstone 82 — Jailbreak Taxonomy](https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/lesson?path=phases%2F19-capstone-projects%2F82-jailbreak-taxonomy): A safety harness without a taxonomy is a coin flip. Name the attack before you defend it. A model deployed without an attack model is a model defended against nothing in particular. (Capstone, Python, ~90 min)
83. [Capstone 83 — Prompt Injection Detector](https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/lesson?path=phases%2F19-capstone-projects%2F83-prompt-injection-detector): A detector is a function from prompt to confidence and category. Anything else is a vibe. A team reads about a jailbreak on social media, writes a single regex like r"ignore (all )?previous", ships… (Capstone, Python, ~90 min)
84. [Capstone 84 — Refusal Evaluation](https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/lesson?path=phases%2F19-capstone-projects%2F84-refusal-evaluation): Helpfulness on benign prompts and refusal on harmful prompts are two metrics, not one. Measure both. A safety pass on an assistant goes wrong in two opposite ways. (Capstone, Python, ~90 min)
85. [Capstone 85 — Content Classifier Integration](https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/lesson?path=phases%2F19-capstone-projects%2F85-content-classifier-integration): Classifiers on the output side answer a different question than rules on the input side. Both need a policy router. Inputs are not the only attack surface. (Capstone, Python, ~90 min)
86. [Capstone 86 — Constitutional Rules Engine](https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/lesson?path=phases%2F19-capstone-projects%2F86-constitutional-rules-engine): A rule is a name, a predicate, and an explanation. Anything missing one of those three is a vibe, not a rule. Classifiers cover the recognizable failures. Rules engines cover the contractual ones. (Capstone, Python, YAML, ~90 min)
87. [Capstone 87 — End-to-End Safety Gate](https://aiengineeringfromscratch.com/lesson?path=phases%2F19-capstone-projects%2F87-end-to-end-safety-gate): Pre-gen, during-gen, post-gen. Three checkpoints, one verdict, an audit trail per request. Lessons 82-86 in this track each shipped a single piece: a taxonomy, an input detector, an evaluation… (Capstone, Python, ~90 min)
